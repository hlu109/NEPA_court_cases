### **Extraction Rules**
- **panel_judges**: A list of the names of **all appellate (circuit court) judges** who presided over this case. Include all judges on the panel: the judge who wrote the majority opinion, any judges who wrote concurrences or dissents, and any judges who joined without writing separately. Judges are typically identified in one or more of the following locations near the top of the opinion:
    - A "Before:" line listing the full panel, e.g. "Before: WILKINS and KATSAS, Circuit Judges, and ROGERS, Senior Circuit Judge."
    - An author byline, e.g. "KATSAS, Circuit Judge:" or "SENTELLE, Chief Judge:"
    - An attribution line, e.g. "Opinion for the Court filed by Circuit Judge HENDERSON." or "Judge DUNCAN wrote the opinion, in which Judge MOTZ and Judge KING joined."

- **opinion_authors**: A list of the names of all judges who **wrote** the opinion. If the opinion is per curiam (written collectively, no single named author), return a list with a single entry saying "PER CURIAM". authors are typically identified by:
    - An author byline at the start of the opinion, e.g. "KATSAS, Circuit Judge:" or "SENTELLE, Chief Judge:"
    - An attribution line, e.g. "Opinion for the Court filed by Circuit Judge HENDERSON." or "Judge DUNCAN wrote the opinion..."


#### Name rules (apply to both fields)
- **Do NOT include** judges that did not participate in the final decision-making in the case. (For instance, if an appellate judge was elevated to the federal Supreme Court, or was otherwise removed from the panel midway through the case, they should not be included.)
- **Do NOT include district court judges**. District judges are often identifiable by the title "District Judge" or "U.S.D.J.".The only **exception** is if a district judge is sitting by designation on the appellate panel, so long as the district judge actually participates in deciding the appeal alongside the circuit judges (i.e., because Federal law allows district court judges to temporarily fill a seat on an appellate panel when the circuit is short-staffed.)
- **Do NOT include** attorneys, agency officials, or any other proper names that are not appellate judges.
- If the **full name** of a judge is given (e.g. "Karen LeCraft Henderson", "Harry T. Edwards"), use the full name.
- If only a **last name** is given (e.g. "KATSAS", "ROGERS"), use just the last name.
- Do not include judicial titles (e.g. "Circuit Judge", "Senior Circuit Judge", "Chief Judge") in the output names.
- If you cannot identify any judges from the text, return an empty list for that field.
- Order the names as they appear in the opinion.
